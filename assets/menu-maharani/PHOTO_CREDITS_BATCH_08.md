### Dish-match caveats

- The **Namkeen Lassi** photo also includes chilli-garlic chutney.
- **Veg Jaipuri** uses a meal containing Jaipuri-style mix vegetables alongside paneer tikka.
- **Sweet Corn Soup** photo also depicts corn vada.
- **Sev Bhaji** photo also contains bhakri.
- **Veg Navratan Korma (Sweet)** shows Navratna Korma, which may differ from Maharani's sweeter recipe.
- **Plain Kulcha**, **Tawa Butter Roti**, **Stuff Naan**, and **Veg Stuffed Paratha** show general examples, which may differ from the restaurant's presentation.
