## A. Terms of use — draft

Bite provides access to participating local restaurant listings, online ordering, restaurant fulfillment information and customer support. The responsible restaurant and delivery/pickup mode will be identified for each order. Availability, service area, menu price, minimum order and delivery charge must be reviewed at checkout. A cart or payment attempt alone is not an accepted restaurant order; successful payment is verified on the trusted server before the order is submitted to the restaurant.

Customers must provide accurate contact and delivery details and may use only accounts/payment methods they are authorized to use. Do not share passwords or disclose a delivery OTP before receiving the food. Bite may pause new ordering for documented misuse while retaining necessary support/order access. Account closure requests are reviewed with outstanding fulfillment, refund and lawful-record obligations.

The restaurant is responsible for accurate menu/availability information and for its agreed food preparation and restaurant-managed delivery/pickup obligations. Bite's allocation of marketplace, food-safety, consumer, privacy, tax and payment responsibilities must be finalized by the legal reviewer; nothing here waives mandatory customer rights or assigns statutory obligations merely by contract.

Customer prices, taxes, discounts and fees must be stated accurately before payment under the approved tax schedule. A material updated checkout total requires a new customer acknowledgement before payment. Orders, cancellations, complaints and refunds follow the approved policies below. Do not promise service outside the displayed area or a guaranteed delivery/payout time without an actual operational basis.

Support/grievance contact, complaint handling, applicable-law/jurisdiction language, lawful limitations and dispute terms must be completed by the reviewer. No broad exclusion of mandatory consumer rights is intended.

## B. Privacy notice — draft

Bite processes account/contact details, selected delivery addresses and optional location, order/fulfillment/support records and restaurant business records to operate the service. Payment processing is performed by Cashfree under its applicable notices; payment credentials and privileged server keys must not appear in Bite's public client or logs.

The selected restaurant receives the customer information necessary for order fulfillment. The restaurant may share necessary delivery details with its own delivery person through the controlled handoff workflow. Customer delivery OTPs must not be embedded in links or shared before delivery. Private restaurant documents and expense bill images use restricted access, not public photo URLs.

Infrastructure includes Supabase for Auth/database/storage and static website hosting; map/reverse-geocoding requests may reach the configured OpenStreetMap/Nominatim service when used. The reviewer must finalize the actual processors, processing locations/contracts, purposes, required consent/notice choices and cross-border treatment. No advertising/profiling or universal background location tracking is represented by this draft.

The client stores interface language, selected PIN, cart and a customer-bound pending-checkout reference locally to support normal use/recovery. It does not intentionally store bank details, address/contact snapshots, payment secrets or delivery OTPs in local storage. Logs and public QA evidence must exclude credentials and private customer/restaurant data.

Customers may request correction, help or account deletion through the available support/account workflow. A deletion request is not an immediate promise to erase financial/order records that must lawfully be retained. Retention periods, access/export/erasure handling, children's-data treatment, consent withdrawal, incident response and grievance escalation must be approved against the applicable Indian legal framework and commencement dates. Do not publish placeholders or invent a retention duration.

## C. Cancellation and refund policy — draft

Before restaurant acceptance, the Customer can request direct cancellation through My Orders. After acceptance, cancellation is a tracked request for restaurant review; preparation status and applicable customer rights must be considered under the approved policy. Delivered orders cannot be cancelled through the ordinary cancellation action, but customers can report quality, missing-item, payment or delivery issues.

Rejected or cancelled paid orders enter a refund review workflow. Administrative approval and provider processing are separate stages. A queued, approved or pending refund is not money received by the customer. Bite shows successful refund status only after trusted provider evidence. Refunds must use the approved original-payment/provider process; processing times and escalation routes must be based on the actual provider and applicable obligations, not an invented fixed number of days.

The five-minute unanswered-order feature is not promised as active until its approved scheduler/configuration passes acceptance. The final published policy must state any applicable charges and prohibited practices, reasons/remedies, response windows and escalation contacts without restricting mandatory consumer rights.

## D. Delivery and pickup policy — draft

Service is limited to the restaurant's enabled PIN/area and delivery/pickup capability. Checkout validates the selected address and applicable delivery charge/minimum. Preparation times are indicative unless a stronger approved commitment is supported. Restaurant closure or paused delivery must be reflected before a new checkout is prepared; existing paid orders require a clear fulfillment/cancellation/support outcome.

The restaurant coordinates its own delivery. Bite does not presently provide a platform driver account/fleet. A secure handoff link carries necessary fulfillment information; the Customer provides the OTP only after the order arrives. If the token/OTP is unavailable, expired or locked, use support rather than bypassing confirmation. Customer contact with delivery is relayed through the restaurant under the current model.

Wrong/missing food, late/failed delivery, unsafe food, inaccessible addresses and pickup failures must follow the approved support/remedy process. Responsibility allocation, packaging/hygiene training, service hours, failed-delivery evidence and lawful charges require owner/reviewer approval.

## E. Restaurant partner / commission / settlement terms — draft

The partner must provide accurate ownership/contact/address, valid applicable licences, menu/prices, hours, delivery coverage and bank/vendor KYC information. Listing review, order-acceptance readiness and payout eligibility are separate checks. A listed partner may not claim that Bite/Cashfree verified a fact without evidence.

Commission is the approved platform default or restaurant override shown in the agreement; the proposed default is 10%, not an unchangeable universal rate. Define the commission base, restaurant-funded versus platform-funded offers, taxes, actual gateway fees, reversals/refunds, support/delivery costs and settlement adjustments explicitly. Gateway fees are not platform profit and may be allocated only under the approved provider/commercial agreement.

Restaurant settlement/payout follows the approved marketplace provider, vendor eligibility, holds/disputes and actual settlement cycle. A software draft, booked commission, payment collection or platform-bank credit does not itself execute a restaurant payout. Do not promise next-day credit unless the provider/agreement actually supports it. No unapproved holding/transfer workaround is authorized.

Finalize food-safety/customer-remedy responsibilities, service standards, data use, audit evidence, suspension/termination, reconciliation/disputes and lawful grievance/consumer rights. Both parties must approve the completed factual/commercial schedule before rollout.
