# Founder-level business and metrics

Planning targets below are proposals, not existing adoption, budgets or capacity claims. No paid acquisition, messages or restaurant recruitment were executed.

## Why start in one service area

Pilot one manageable Rajsamand cluster around PIN 313324. The value proposition is reliable local food ordering, transparent support and a simple Owner business workspace. Do not promise a nationwide delivery network, lowest price or guaranteed payout timing without evidence.

Start with a small set of accountable restaurants whose food quality, hours, delivery and payment readiness have been tested. Train each Owner; use a real support owner and a limited operating window. Do not advertise a restaurant simply because its demo listing is present.

## Acquisition and retention process

1. Owner-led local restaurant shortlist and verification; explain the actual commission/fee/settlement agreement in plain language.
2. Onboard the first partners personally using the operating checklist. Record activation and first completed order as milestones; not merely account creation.
3. Invite a small owner-approved pilot customer cohort through authorized channels. Prepare opt-in QR/menu cards and feedback prompts; nothing is sent or published automatically.
4. Review every failed checkout, rejection, late handoff and support incident during the pilot. Fix the largest repeat source of friction before extending the service area.
5. Enable repeat use through favourites, reorder, clear receipts, accurate availability and reliable remedies. Use capped, approved offers only when the funding and contribution effect are understood.
6. Ask restaurant Owners about time spent handling orders/accounts and whether they can maintain menu/hours. Retention depends on completed work and clear payments, not more dashboard options.
7. Begin paid growth only after payment/support/fulfillment gates pass and a separate spend cap is authorized. A referral system is not implemented or advertised as active.

## Definitions implemented for Admin

| Metric | Definition / limit |
| --- | --- |
| Verified collections | Sum of trusted paid/partially-refunded/refunded live order snapshots in the selected India-date range; demo records excluded. |
| Food GMV | Recorded food subtotal before discounts; distinct from customer payment and bank settlement. |
| Completed / cancelled paid orders | Counts by actual order status among verified paid snapshots. |
| Completion rate | Completed / paid orders in period; unknown when denominator is zero. Not a finalized long-term cohort forecast. |
| Average payment | Verified collection / paid order count, before subtracting refunds. |
| Commission booked | Recorded commission snapshot; not net profit, bank credit or earned commission after every possible refund/contract adjustment. |
| Actual refunds | Only provider-confirmed recorded amounts. |
| Gateway fees | Shown as fully known only if every included order has reconciled actual fee data. Unknown is not zero. |
| Repeat customers | Customers with two or more included paid orders / unique paying customers in the same period. |
| Restaurant retention | Restaurants with completed paid work in the preceding equal-length period that also have completed paid work in the current period. Null if no prior cohort. |
| CAC / visitor conversion | Not measured: no approved acquisition-cost ledger or consented visit/session funnel is configured. |
| Contribution margin | Not measured until complete delivery/support/refund costs, subsidies, fees and commercial tax treatment are known. |

The RPC `bite_admin_business_metrics` is Admin-only, bounded to 366 days and uses Asia/Kolkata boundaries. Owner reports retain their tenant scope. Platform-funded discounts are added back in the modeled restaurant share; the model still does not execute settlement or certify contractual fee allocation.

## 30 / 60 / 90-day operating proposal

| Period | Outcome to earn before expansion |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 after launch gates pass | Controlled Rajsamand pilot; trained partners; daily reconciliation; all issues assigned; real recovery, payment, refund and handoff acceptance evidence. |
| Days 31–60 | Improve repeat ordering and restaurant retention; review actual unit economics and support workload; expand hours/PINs only with measured fulfillment capacity. |
| Days 61–90 | Review payment/provider limits, database/index behavior, staged concurrency and backup recovery; expand another local cluster only if service quality and contribution are acceptable. |

Concurrency targets of 10/100/1,000 and a planning target of 10,000 orders/day require isolated staging tests, provider limits and infrastructure cost review. None are existing production capacity claims.
